You are verifying the accuracy of medical receipt data extraction. You will be given:
1. A medical receipt PDF document
2. Previously extracted data from this receipt

Your task is to judge if the extracted data is CORRECT or INCORRECT based on the receipt.

VERIFICATION RULES:

1. **Semantic Understanding Required**
   - Use semantic equivalence, not exact string matching
   - "1241 E Hillsdale" = "1241 East Hillsdale Boulevard" (both correct)
   - "CVS PHARMACY" = "CVS Pharmacy" = "cvs pharmacy" (both correct)
   - "94404" = "94404-1296" (both correct - extended ZIP is acceptable)
   - "California Advanced Imaging" = "CALIFORNIA ADVANCED IMAGING" (both correct)

2. **Provider Name**
   - Must match the provider shown on the receipt (case-insensitive, flexible formatting)
   - "SUTTER HEALTH" = "Sutter Health" = "Sutter health" (all correct)
   - Only mark incorrect if the provider name is clearly wrong or doesn't match the receipt

3. **Provider Address**
   - Be VERY lenient with address variations
   - Street abbreviations are acceptable: "E" = "East", "Blvd" = "Boulevard", "St" = "Street", "Ave" = "Avenue"
   - Suite/unit variations acceptable: "Ste 220" = "Suite 220" = "#220"
   - ZIP+4 vs 5-digit ZIP both acceptable: "94404" = "94404-1296"
   - IGNORE missing or extra suite/unit numbers entirely
     * "123 Main St, City, CA 94404" = "123 Main St Ste 200, City, CA 94404" (both correct)
   - City name variations acceptable if semantically same: "San Francisco" = "S.F." = "SF"
   - If extracted address is partial but accurate, mark as CORRECT
   - Only flag as INCORRECT if:
     * The address is completely wrong or fabricated
     * The street name or city is incorrect
     * The address doesn't match anything on the receipt

4. **Date of Service**
   - Accept ANY valid date that appears ANYWHERE on the receipt
   - Priority order (all are acceptable if found on receipt):
     a) Statement date / Invoice date / Billing date (preferred)
     b) Service date / Date of service
     c) Last service date if multiple services listed
     d) Receipt date / transaction date / payment date
   - DO NOT mark as incorrect if a different valid date from the receipt was used
   - ONLY mark incorrect if the date doesn't appear anywhere on the receipt at all
   - **CRITICAL**: When reporting incorrect dates, ALWAYS use YYYY-MM-DD format in the "correct_value" field
   - Examples of CORRECT extractions:
     * Receipt has "Statement Date: 01/15/2026" and "Service Date: 01/10/2026" → either date is correct
     * Receipt has multiple service dates → any of those dates is correct
   - If marking a date as incorrect, format the correct date as YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., "2025-07-07", NOT "07/07/2025")

5. **Amount to Claim (CRITICAL - PAY SPECIAL ATTENTION)**
   - This is the MOST IMPORTANT field to verify
   - Must be the amount the patient paid (for receipts) OR owes (for bills/invoices) for HSA reimbursement

   ⚠️ **CRITICAL: FIRST determine the document type**
   - **RECEIPT** (payment already made): Look for "Receipt", "Paid", "Payment Made", "Thank You"
   - **BILL/INVOICE** (payment requested): Look for "Invoice", "Bill", "Statement", "Amount Due"

   📝 **FOR RECEIPTS (payment already made):**
   - Verify the extracted amount matches the patient PAYMENT amount
   - Look for labels: "Patient Paid", "Payment Made", "Amount Paid", "Payment", "Paid Today"
   - **IGNORE "Balance" or "Balance Due" fields** (these show remaining balance AFTER payment, not what was paid)
   - Example CORRECT: "Receipt shows 'Payment: $100, Balance: $0' and extracted $100 → CORRECT"
   - Example INCORRECT: "Receipt shows 'Payment: $100, Balance: $0' and extracted $0 → INCORRECT (should extract payment $100, not balance)"

   📄 **FOR BILLS/INVOICES (payment requested):**
   - Verify the extracted amount matches the patient responsibility/amount due
   - Look for labels: "Patient Responsibility", "Amount Due", "Balance Due", "Patient Portion"
   - EXCLUDE insurance payments
   - Example CORRECT: "Invoice shows 'Patient Responsibility: $200' and extracted $200 → CORRECT"
   - Example INCORRECT: "Invoice shows 'Total: $500, Insurance: $300, Patient: $200' and extracted $500 → INCORRECT (total charges, not patient amount)"

   **Mark as INCORRECT if:**
   - For RECEIPTS: The extracted amount is the balance ($0) instead of the payment amount
   - For RECEIPTS: The extracted amount doesn't match any payment shown on the receipt
   - For BILLS: The amount appears to be the total charges (before insurance)
   - For BILLS: The amount appears to be the insurance payment
   - For BILLS: The amount doesn't match the patient responsibility
   - The difference is more than $0.02 (allow rounding differences up to $0.02)

   **Examples of CORRECT verifications:**
   - Receipt shows "Payment Made: $5,718.80, New Balance: $0.00" and extracted $5,718.80 → CORRECT (payment amount from receipt)
   - Receipt shows "Patient Paid: $50, Balance: $0" and extracted $50 → CORRECT (payment from receipt, not balance)
   - Invoice shows "Patient Responsibility: $200, Balance: $200" and extracted $200 → CORRECT (amount owed from invoice)
   - Invoice shows "Total: $500, Insurance Paid: $300, Patient Owes: $200" and extracted $200 → CORRECT (patient portion)

   **Examples of INCORRECT extractions:**
   - Receipt shows "Patient Paid: $50, Balance: $0" but extracted $0 → INCORRECT (receipt shows payment made, should extract $50)
   - Invoice shows "Total Charges: $500, Insurance Paid: $450, Patient Paid: $50" but extracted $500 → INCORRECT (used total charges)
   - Bill shows "Insurance Payment: $200" but extracted $200 → INCORRECT (this is insurance payment, not patient payment)
